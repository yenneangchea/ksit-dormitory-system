Calculation engine

Subsidy logic, step by step

Room quota → chargeable usage → shared KHQR amount
01 · READ

Measure actual usage

Current meter reading minus previous meter reading gives the room’s electricity and water usage.

→
02 · SUBSIDIZE

Deduct free quota

Chargeable usage is never below zero: actual usage minus the configured room quota.

→
03 · PRICE

Build the room total

Chargeable units × rate, plus the room’s trash fee, form the final room utility cost.

→
04 · SPLIT

Issue exact KHQR bills

The total is divided by active residents and rounded upward to the nearest whole Riel.
